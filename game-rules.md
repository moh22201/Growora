# Growora — approved game rules

This file records the current approved rules. Do not change the values without the owner's explicit approval.

## Currency and wallets
- One in-game currency displayed as `$`.
- Verified deposit: 1 USDT credits 1 `$` to the play wallet.
- Minimum deposit: 1 USDT.
- Play wallet is used for seeds, field unlocks, assets, rods, and operating fees.
- Sales wallet receives proceeds from selling crops, fish, honey, and eggs.
- Only the sales wallet counts toward withdrawal eligibility.
- Minimum withdrawal: 5 USDT.
- Withdrawal fee: 10%.
- Network: BNB Smart Chain / BEP-20.
- Withdrawal funds remain reserved until an administrator records payout confirmation and TXID.
- Public withdrawal ticker displays confirmed withdrawals only.

## Permanent assets
One per account, one-time purchase, affects new timers only:
- Farming: $1,000, crop growth time reduced by 15%.
- Fishing: $500, fishing wait reduced by 15%.
- Beehive: $300, honey production time reduced by 15%.
- Chicken: $300, egg production time reduced by 15%.

## Fishing
- Fishing area unlock: $5 one-time.
- Ordinary rod: $0.15, 5 uses, wait 5–15 min, cooldown 20 min.
- Upgraded rod: $0.50, 7 uses, wait 4–12 min, cooldown 15 min.
- Legendary rod: $1.60, 10 uses, wait 3–10 min, cooldown 10 min.
- Collect deadline: 60 seconds. If the fish escapes, one rod use is consumed without refund.
- No free daily attempts in V1. Fishing asset reduces wait time, not odds.
- A legendary fish can be caught once per account; later legendary rolls convert to gold fish.

## Beehive
- Purchase: $3 once.
- Operating fee: $0.20 per 24-hour cycle.
- Produces 2 honey per cycle, one every 12 hours.
- Honey sale price: $0.60 each.
- Products expire 6 hours after cycle end if uncollected.
- Collect before restarting and paying the fee again.
- No feed cost.

## Chicken coop
- Purchase: $5 once.
- Operating fee: $0.40 per 24-hour cycle.
- Produces 6 eggs per cycle, 2 every 8 hours.
- Egg sale price: $0.35 each.
- Eggs expire 6 hours after cycle end if uncollected.
- Collect before restarting and paying the fee again.
- No feed cost.
